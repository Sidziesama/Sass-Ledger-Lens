## 90-Second Flow

1. Start on the September close review.
2. State the AI boundary: GIDE/Ornith proposes narratives; LedgerLens verifies them.
3. Show the CFO brief: only verified or qualified claims are allowed in.
4. Click Revenue and show the rejected claims:
   - broad growth rejected when concentration is too high
   - pricing-cause rejected because transactions do not establish causation
5. Open Evidence and show the graph:
   - summary tie-out
   - driver math
   - cited source rows
   - duplicate quarantine
6. Open Business memory:
   - context can qualify an explanation
   - stale or unapproved memory cannot drive a claim
7. Open Test lab and show all controlled edge cases passing.
8. Open PRISM and show live tracking connected from the same courtroom engine.

## Differentiation

Most finance AI explains numbers.

LedgerLens refuses to explain until every claim proves itself against source rows.

## Pitch Line

LedgerLens is an evidence courtroom for finance AI: GIDE proposes the narrative, LedgerLens ranks material movements, drills into the real drivers, rejects weak claims, and publishes only source-backed CFO language.

## Judge Questions

If asked whether it trains a model:

No. We use GIDE/Ornith as the local AI proposal layer. The model can propose a narrative, but the courtroom engine decides whether the sentence is allowed into the CFO brief.

If asked why it uses PRISM:

PRISM proves the reliability loop. LedgerLens sends the review, courtroom, and edge-lab traces so failures can be observed, improved, and demonstrated.

If asked why this wins over a normal variance chatbot:

A chatbot gives a plausible answer. LedgerLens gives a defensible answer, and rejects itself when the data is not strong enough.
